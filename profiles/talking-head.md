# Talking Head

Look for strong openings, repeated phrases, dead air, mistakes, useful emphasis and natural personality.

Use restrained jump cuts. Preserve meaningful pauses and context.



Motion guidance:
- Start with clean framing and restrained movement.
- Use a callout only when a spoken phrase deserves emphasis.
- Use cutaway footage when it directly illustrates what is being said.
- Do not cover the speaker's face just to make the frame busier.
