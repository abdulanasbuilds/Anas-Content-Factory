# Showreel

Use a more energetic visual rhythm for promotional/event montage work.

Priorities:
1. Story and emotion
2. Visual variety
3. Music/audio rhythm
4. Branding
5. Pacing
6. Effects

Motion:
- Use stronger beat changes at meaningful moments.
- Prefer real discovered assets and event footage over generic decoration.
- Keep the brand visually consistent from beginning to end.
- Do not use motion to hide weak source material.
