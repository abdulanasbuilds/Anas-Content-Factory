# Course

Prioritize comprehension and retention without making the lesson visually noisy.

Priorities:
1. Understanding
2. Structure
3. Pacing
4. Audio
5. Visual aids
6. Branding
7. Effects

Motion:
- Use short takeaway callouts.
- Prefer simple diagrams, labels or cutaways over decorative motion.
- Keep the speaker visible when explanation matters.
- Use a full-screen visual only when it genuinely improves understanding.
