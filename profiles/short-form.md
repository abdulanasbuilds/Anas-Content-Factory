# Short-Form

Use a faster rhythm than long-form, but keep the edit readable.

Priorities:
1. Hook and standalone clarity
2. Pacing
3. Clean captions
4. Purposeful visual changes
5. Audio
6. Branding
7. Effects

Motion:
- Favor sparse but noticeable callouts around important phrases.
- Use cutaway clips or screenshots when they directly illustrate the sentence.
- Avoid changing visuals on every word.
- The opening seconds can carry stronger visual emphasis than the middle.
